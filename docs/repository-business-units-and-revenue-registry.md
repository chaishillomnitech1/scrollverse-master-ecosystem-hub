# ScrollVerse Repository Business Units & Revenue Registry

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-14  
**Inventory basis:** 99 repositories returned by the connected GitHub account inventory.  
**Registry file:** `registry/repository-business-units.v1.json`

## Operating principle

Every repository is treated as an independently addressable operating unit: its own purpose, identity, products or services, customer path, revenue attribution, operational owner, and evidence trail. Units can share the ScrollVerse platform and infrastructure while retaining independent identity and capability.

A repository is not automatically a separate incorporated company. The registry deliberately separates the **operating-unit identity** from the **legal-entity identity**. Legal formation, jurisdiction, tax registrations, bank accounts, licenses, and contracting authority are recorded only when supported by source evidence.

## Revenue model

Each unit can earn revenue through its own approved mix of:
- direct-to-customer physical goods and services;
- digital products, media, books, licenses, memberships, and learning;
- creator royalties and rights-based licensing;
- software, systems, APIs, integrations, and enterprise services;
- approved on-chain digital goods and customer-directed wallet settlement;
- marketplace participation and partner distribution.

Revenue attribution is kept separate from settlement proof. An order, invoice, internal ledger entry, token balance, or projection does not prove cleared funds. The registry only advances to `reconciled_revenue_verified` after order records, provider events, and settlement evidence match.

## Customer choice and provider independence

The shared commerce fabric exposes replaceable payment routes rather than forcing every unit into one processor. Each unit may select approved card processors, bank/ACH partners, storefront providers, or customer-controlled wallet routes. Fees, currency, settlement destination, delivery, refunds, and support are disclosed before a customer confirms.

The platform owns catalog, order orchestration, route selection, receipts, ledger, reconciliation, reporting, and support workflows. Banks, acquirers, ACH networks, and wallet infrastructure remain external settlement rails unless and until the operating company has the required authorization and infrastructure to provide those functions itself.

## Registry status rules

- `catalogued_not_activated`: repository exists in the inventory; business workflow is not yet verified.
- `not_yet_classified`: purpose is not inferred from the repository name alone.
- `not_assessed`: product, revenue, payment, and public-commerce status require repository-level evidence.
- `not_verified`: no legal-entity status is inferred from a GitHub repository.
- `preserve_existing_extend_without_overwrite`: sigils, logos, READMEs, source, history, and original artwork remain intact; new identity assets are additive.

## Preservation

This registry is additive. It does not rename repositories, replace READMEs, overwrite source, alter visibility, change deployment settings, or declare any business, product, provider, or financial capability live without evidence.
