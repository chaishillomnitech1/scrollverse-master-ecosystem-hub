Kun Fayakun · authorized canon

Generated material and community proposals remain proposals until authorized. Canon changes are traceable, attributable, versioned, and reversible through appended corrections—not silent erasure.

Production loop

1. Signals & triage

Audience feedback, repository changes, incidents, analytics, creator direction, and business requirements enter with source, timestamp, identifier, confidence, sensitivity, and provenance.

2. Specialist production

Route scoped tasks to independent creative, lore, research, engineering, archive, commerce, and assurance contributors. Coordination does not collapse identities or authorship.

3. Review & release

Human approval gates govern canon, public media, payments, legal/financial claims, contracts, token operations, and production deployment.

4. Monitor & learn

Capture build/test results, deployment IDs, customer outcomes, incidents, corrections, and follow-up signals. New learning appends to the record without rewriting history.

Machine-readable foundation

Schema registry

  • Signal schema — observation separated from interpretation.
  • Agent task schema — identity, scope, permissions, provenance, outputs, and review.
  • Canon event schema — proposal, review, decree, propagation, correction, rejection.
  • Artifact manifest schema — source lineage, hashes, rights, preservation, publication state.
  • Proof record schema — claim, evidence, verification status, and source references.
